## What this phase is for


forge's core claim is that no code ships without surviving three
consecutive clean review cycles. A sequence of defects -- three on the
mutation-gate branch alone, plus receipt-timestamp and infra-anchor
poison -- showed that "ran and found nothing" and "never ran" were
indistinguishable in several places, and that a green verdict could
coexist with a gate that had measured nothing.

This phase closes those gaps. The charter lists 10 items; this file
organises them into 3 execution chains and records the 5 backlog todos
that fall outside the charter's scope.

## Process change (adopted in forge phase planning)

Every plan item that adds a gate, check, or measurement states its
**silent-failure signature**: what the output looks like when it runs
and finds nothing, beside what it looks like when it never ran. If
those two are identical, that is a design defect to resolve before
implementing.

Sharpened form (evidence spans forge + OmniRoute): if a change produces
a signal, name its consumer -- a signal with a scheduled consumer and a
signal with no consumer look identical on disk.

Not promoted to ~/CLAUDE.md global until it catches something in a
third project (charter's own route; forge + OmniRoute are the first
two).
